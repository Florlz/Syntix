## How to use this handbook

This file has two layers:

1. **Operational handbook** - a reviewed reference for designing judge and tabulator workflows.
2. **Raw OCR appendix** - the complete page-by-page extraction, retained for audit and text search.

The approved PDF is always the controlling source. References such as `p. 19` mean the PDF page, not the page number printed inside the proposal. A value marked **Conflict** or **Unresolved** must not become an active scoring rule until an authorized event administrator confirms it.

### Reliability labels

| Label | Meaning | Dashboard treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Legible and internally consistent in the source | May be configured, with its PDF page reference retained |
| **Conflict** | The proposal prints two incompatible values or rules | Block publishing until an authorized value is recorded |
| **Unresolved** | A required value is missing, malformed, or not safely recoverable | Do not infer or auto-complete it |
| **OCR-only** | Present in the extraction but not manually verified against the scan | Use only as a search aid |

## 4. Eligibility, validation, and protests

- A participant must be officially enrolled and must present the required school identification and event credentials. Participant details must match the approved entry and roster (pp. 3-4, 11, 19).
- A student may participate in a maximum of three events, subject to the source's event-specific restrictions (p. 3).
- Former varsity players may not participate in the same event represented during the preceding two years, as stated by the proposal (p. 3).
- Sports teams must be in proper uniform. Socio-cultural participants must report to the backstage or holding area 30 minutes before their contest; failure to appear when called is treated as non-competing (pp. 11, 19).
- Drawing of lots occurs during the final meeting. Sports and other events follow game succession unless the organizing committee announces an authorized change (pp. 4, 11, 19).
- Referee and Board of Judges decisions are final, subject to the stated protest process. A protest must be submitted in writing to the adjudication committee within six hours and carries a PHP 5,000 protest fee (pp. 4, 11, 19).

For implementation, preserve the approved roster snapshot, validation status, officials, timestamps, raw scores, deductions, computed totals, placements, protests, approvals, and every subsequent correction in an audit log.

## 12. Dashboard implementation contract

Each configured event should carry its source page, reliability label, division, participants, officials, scoring mode, criteria or match structure, time rules, deductions, tie-break policy, general point schedule, approval state, and version history.

Judge screens should expose only the assigned criterion inputs and applicable deductions. Tabulator screens should preserve each judge's raw score, show weight calculations, surface missing scores and conflicts, require an authorized approval before publishing, and lock published results behind an auditable correction workflow. Values marked **Conflict** or **Unresolved** in this handbook must remain disabled until confirmed.
